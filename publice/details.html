DILSHAN-MD Bot — Full Details

A self-hosted WhatsApp automation bot built on the Baileys multi-device API. Runs 24/7 on your own deployment, fully customizable, no third-party control over your account.

    Anti-link / Anti-badword — auto-moderation for non-admins
  • 🚫
    Anti-tag / Anti-call / Anti-delete — extra group protection
  • 🤖

  • 🎨

    Presence automation — auto-read, auto-typing, auto-status view
  • 👥

  • 🧩

  • 📢

🚀How Deployment Works

  • 1️⃣ You buy a plan and link your WhatsApp number via the Pair/QR tab.
  • 2️⃣ We deploy your own dedicated bot instance — not a shared/multi-tenant bot.
  • 3️⃣ Your plan's expiry date is set automatically based on what you bought (1/3/5 months).
  • 4️⃣ We'll remind you a few days before your plan expires so you can renew without downtime.
  • 5️⃣ If a plan lapses, the bot pauses automatically — renew anytime to bring it back online.

☁️Hosting Infrastructure

  • 🌐 Deployed on production-grade cloud infrastructure with automatic restarts if the process ever drops.
  • 💾 Persistent session storage — no re-scanning QR codes after routine restarts.
  • 📈 Infrastructure sized to handle multiple bots reliably, so your bot isn't competing for resources.
  • 🔒 Your WhatsApp session stays private — never shared with other customers' bots.

🏗️Good to Know

  • 🛠️ Fully customizable — commands, replies, and behavior can be changed anytime.
  • 📦 Files up to WhatsApp's 2GB limit send directly; anything larger auto-delivers as a download link instead.
  • ⚠️ Uses WhatsApp's unofficial Web API — carries a ban/rate-limit risk like any unofficial automation tool. Use responsibly, no spam or bulk messaging.
